Our Story

Food that brings
people together

We are a trendy, modern Restaurant & Tapas Bar set against the lush fairways of the Kimiad Golf Course in Moreleta Park, Pretoria. Our kitchen is inspired by a genuine love of travel and good eating — flavours that cross continents, served in a setting that feels like home.

With our tapas — shared plates made for lingering — we create an atmosphere where every meal is a gathering. Whether you're after a leisurely farmhouse breakfast, hand-rolled pizza at lunch, or a round of cocktails at sunset, there is always a seat at our table. Families, golf groups, and corporate celebrations are all equally welcome.
